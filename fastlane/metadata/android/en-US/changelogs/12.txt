* Fixed OsmAnd navigation: OsmAnd only accepted the "navigate route" command while its own map
  screen was already open (a quirk of its API) - the app now brings OsmAnd to the foreground
  first and retries the call until that succeeds, instead of requiring OsmAnd to be opened
  manually beforehand
* The route map and the map picker now properly free their memory when you leave them
* Multi-waypoint routes compute faster (legs are requested in parallel instead of one after
  another)
